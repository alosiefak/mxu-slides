SQLite: the database library MxU Slides for Windows uses to read OpenLP's songs
===============================================================================

"Import from OpenLP" reads OpenLP's songs.sqlite and custom.sqlite. Three pieces of other
people's work do the reading. They come from nuget.org as the package Microsoft.Data.Sqlite and
the packages it depends on; nothing here was built or changed by MxU.
